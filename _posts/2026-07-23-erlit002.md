## ERLIT Chapter 2

*Beep, beep, beep, beep, beep…*

The sound, repeating with regularity, irritated my ears. I slowly opened my eyes to see a white ceiling. My eyes were hurting from the brightness of the fluorescent lights hanging from the ceiling. I squinted my eyes and took a look at my surroundings. It wasn’t my room. It wasn’t anywhere I recognised. It was a small room surrounded by unknown machines.

Alarmed, I tried to lift myself up, but there was something stuck to my finger. I looked up to see a small monitor after following the wires that were fastened to the pincers. I’d seen something similar in dramas. The ones where the patient’s electrocardiogram would be drawn with zigzags, and a long *beep* sound would signify death. I put my hand on my chest. There was no way to be sure, but it seemed like my heart was fine. I tossed and turned for a bit, but nothing felt particularly painful.

I raised myself, but I had to stop breathing for a moment. I was naked. There were machines hanging all over my body. I couldn’t get a hold of the situation, wondering why I was in this state. I carefully searched through my memory and soon remembered the robbers breaking into the bank. I also calmly thought of the man who called me an ability user. My university tuition... I sighed when I reached that point. It was too late. I had already paid for the tuition at the other university I had been accepted to in the first round, so I had no choice but to go there. There was barely any difference between the two, but it was still a place I wanted to go to more. I ruffled my hair with my hand.

It seemed like I was doing an ability examination at S.E. right now since they had called me an ability user. It was more thorough than the one that was required for the entire nation. It was ridiculous. Even if it was entirely at the discretion of the military, a place that managed ability users, it wasn’t even certain that I was an ability user yet, so what on earth were they doing, bringing me here in a manner that was on par with a kidnapping? I had a good chance of winning if I sued them for preventing me from attending university, but if I wanted to sue them or anything else, I had to leave this place first. To get out, I’d have to remove the machines from my body, and I didn’t know what they were, so I was too anxious to remove them on my own.

“Excuse me.”

I let out a noise and called out to someone, though I wasn’t sure who I was calling for either. I was pretty sure there was no one in the small room, so I called a little louder, hoping there might be someone in the hallway.

“Is anyone there?”

No response came, no matter how many times I called. I looked around for an emergency call bell or anything similar, but there was nothing of the sort. I sighed. When they confirm that I’m not an ability user, they’ll send me home. Anyhow, no one would worry about me being late, and no one would realise I hadn’t been home for a few days. It would take at least a month for my father to notice my absence and report me missing... I had called him just the day before yesterday. Everything would likely be sorted out before then. By the way, I’d like to cover up down there... I looked around my surroundings again, but the only piece of cloth I could find was the bed sheet.

“Hngh...?”

The more time passed, the more something felt strange. Bit by bit, I couldn’t breathe. The sensation of taking in air was strange. It was a colourless, odourless gas, but the more I breathed in, the more I choked, so it wasn’t normal oxygen. My vision became blurry, and I heard a long *beep–* sound, just like in those dramas. I thought my heart was fine, but it felt like it was stopping. What is this? Was I going to die before even getting a chance to go to university? Before the *beep–* stopped, my vision went black.

I opened my eyes again. I didn’t know how many damn times it’d been. This time, I found myself submerged in a jelly-like liquid, with a respirator clamped over my mouth and nose, forcing oxygen into my lungs. It was oxygen now, but I was afraid it could turn into some unknown gas at any moment again.

I had no idea how many days had passed, and during my unconsciousness, I hadn’t eaten or drunk anything, yet I didn’t feel hungry or thirsty. Whatever I did while unconscious, I didn’t even want to go to the bathroom. I was just being kept naked and immersed in some strange experimental devices. Were ability measurement exams always this prolonged and laborious?

“Ngh!”

I couldn’t groan properly because my respiratory system was blocked, but I felt an intense electric shock coursing through my entire body before it stopped. They seemed to have found my limit this time, ensuring I wouldn’t pass out. My consciousness didn’t fade. I trembled and shivered in the aftermath of the electric shock, but the puttering culture medium quickly absorbed the shock.

I wasn’t sure what the purpose of this experiment was, but it seemed like they were trying to find the point at which I could withstand it. All the while, they protected me from dying, just in case. It truly was an impossible, ridiculous, and preposterous experiment. It was a complete violation of experimental ethics. They didn’t have my consent, and even if they did, they had no right to subject me to such life-threatening experiments. But I couldn’t argue back. I hadn’t even seen a shadow of a human being here. These fucking bastards—this might be S.E., but…

I could no longer think after the shock that shot through my body again.

A flash of lightning struck just above my head, powerful enough for it to be visible. It struck me right on the crown of my head, but I only slowly closed and opened my eyes. The mock battle room. The only place I could see people. No, a place where I could recognise the presence of people. They could see me, but I couldn’t see them.

Within this room with rapidly changing holographic backgrounds, I was subject to dozens of different attacks. I admit it now. The fact that I am an ability user. Because no normal person would survive being struck by a current that could be tens of who knows how many thousand volts. But that didn’t mean I agreed with the reason they kept me here. It was because the only currents I didn’t react to were those made by ability users. I reacted the same way as a normal person when I was struck by a normal electric current running through a wire.

I had experienced burning my hands while boiling ramen, but I remained unscathed by the fire generated by an ability user. It may seem decent, but it was actually useless. If you locked me in a room and started a fire using abilities, I would suffocate to death from the smoke produced by burning other things.

Abilities couldn’t be used to make me float. However, if someone were to make the ground I was standing on float up, my body would naturally be lifted as well. If they break the ground after making it rise about fifty metres, I’ll fall to my death by the laws of gravity, breaking my neck bone or spine.

I couldn’t feel the sensation of someone digging into my head. My body did not move on its own accord, as it was obeying someone else’s will. It wasn’t my will, however, to have to sit still here and now. I desperately wanted to go back home, a place I’ve never missed in my life, but I couldn’t.

An ability user on whom abilities do not work. The words of the man I met at the bank come to mind. Yeah, let’s say I am, but I still didn’t know what use that was. It was just that abilities didn’t work on me; if I get shot in a vital area, I’ll die. Even if I were instead wounded in a non-critical area, I’d die if I lost a lethal amount of blood. These bastards have tried everything they could think of to kill me, not just abilities but knives, needles, and even poison.

I didn’t know whether this was a bad or good thing, but I was still alive and well. Just as they couldn’t damage my body with their abilities, they couldn’t heal me with their abilities either, but even without healing-type abilities, S.E.’s medical technology was remarkable. I’ve read rumours and speculative articles on the internet claiming that the S.E. had made medical advances unrivalled by the rest of the world after decades of unethical bio-experimentation. At the time, I assumed it was just the usual false propaganda geared against the government, but the article had actually been watered down.

The conclusion of all the experiments conducted so far was that all destructive weapons, with the exception of abilities, worked on me, and apart from being immune to abilities, I had no other ability. So, I was an ability user, but I didn’t have any combat abilities. Therefore, I wasn’t needed in the military. Following the final conclusion that was naturally laid out, they should’ve let me go, but they didn’t. Why?

I looked at the black window. There must have been people beyond that window. People toying with me. I wanted to kill them, but I didn’t have the ability to kill. Now, there were too many things that I didn’t have. I didn’t even have clothes. I’ve been naked for months. Well, they don’t put clothes on animals when they test them.

As I sighed softly, a chill ran down my spine. Water was rising from beneath me. Feeling the dampness of it, it really was water. Wouldn’t they know by now, without even having to experiment, that if I was submerged in water, I’d drown? Wasn’t it time for them to draw an inductive conclusion after having accumulated enough examples?

However, the rapidly rising water eventually engulfed my face. They weren’t going to kill me, but they weren’t going to interfere while I was on the brink of death either. Were they trying to see if I’d awaken some kind of sealed ability when I’m faced with the crisis of death, like in those manhwas? I’d rather that be the case too, so that I could beat the shit out of all of them. But no matter how many times I’ve come close to death, I’ve never triggered any kind of ability. Because I don’t have one. I don’t have any abilities!

Because I was submerged in water, I thought I could cry a little and no one would notice, but tears wouldn’t even come out now.

“Here, your ID card.”

They finally provided me with clothes to wear. I was sitting on a normal chair, listening to the words of a normal-looking woman. Although I could see a human being at last, I wasn’t feeling happy. My eyes fixed on the small card placed before me. My photo and name were familiar, but I couldn’t see it as my ID card. Because I had never consented to becoming a member of this place.

“I have no intention of becoming a soldier.”

“Rather than a soldier, it’s more of a special position? We’re affiliated with the military, so the rank and organisation structure are similar, but we’re...”

“Whatever it is, I’m not doing it.”

“Ability users must be in the military. You know the law, don’t you?”

“No, what use is my ability in the military? How can a government organisation do this to someone without their consent...”

“We did receive consent, though?”

“...What?”

“From your guardian. You’re still under the age of nineteen internationally, so your guardian’s consent is required.”

The woman in the white gown laughed and said, “We’re not such a lawless bunch.” My guardian. I thought of my father. Yeah, if it were my father, he could have consented. No, come to think of it, he’d be thrilled to. Why hadn’t I thought that far?

“Anyhow, your name has been added to the list. You do know that the list of ability users is shared among countries worldwide, right? This isn’t a situation in which everything is decided simply because you don’t want to. Don’t you know that we’ll give you a good annual salary? A hazard pay as well.”

“...Ha...”

“Or maybe you don’t need a high salary because you’re a rich young master? Anyway, with a gold spoon[^1]...”

I kicked the table in front of me with a loud thud, and simultaneously, the muzzle of a gun came to my head. The security guard standing behind me had a gun pointed at the back of my head. I was handcuffed, so I couldn’t resist anymore. No, even if I tried to resist, I had no abilities I could use...

“Then you should have said so from the beginning! You did all sorts of experiments on me without explaining a single thing, and now you want me to work here? If you were me, would you agree?”

“We were unsure if you were an ability user. We’ve never seen anything like you before. We can’t explain these kinds of things to civilians, and what are you going to do now if you’re not going to do it? Are you going to refuse enlistment and go to prison?”

I wanted to punch her for being so laid-back. I would rather have a real, decent ability; then, I wouldn’t be feeling this unfair. What kind of state was I in, having my life taken hostage for some ability that didn’t even seem like an ability? I lowered my head and calmed my anger, and the woman slipped the ID card into the front pocket of the shirt I was wearing.

“Use of violence and abusive language towards a superior. Three days in military prison.”

I jerked my head up. Who decided she was my boss? I didn’t even belong here. How was kicking a table violence? What they did to me was kidnapping, imprisonment, and attempted murder! I didn’t even swear, so what abusive language? Not just prison, but military prison?

“Hey! You crazy—!”

“Take him away.”

As the woman’s words fell, the men in black forced me to my feet. I tried to shake their hands off, but I had no choice but to be dragged away by the strength of their grip. I grabbed whatever I could get my hands on and threw it, screaming, “Let me go! You fucking bastards, I didn’t fucking do anything wrong!”

However, the woman just sat cross-legged and watched the scene with a relaxed air. I was so enraged at the sight that I spat out every curse that came to mind, but the woman spoke nonchalantly to the men grabbing me, “He’ll have to stay there for a week.”

I was led down a long corridor by the men’s hands, into an iron elevator, and then down somewhere. Afterwards, they locked me up in some place. The door slid shut in front of me. I kicked at the door with a loud thud, but it didn’t make the slightest scratch.

The questions that had been festering inside me lately about where I was and why I had to do this amplified to their highest levels, but no one gave me an answer. There was only unilateral information and coercion. I roughly ran a hand through my hair and screamed, but nothing changed.

After a while, I was able to breathe more easily. Only then could I look around my surroundings, realising I was confined within a space so small that if I were to take two steps away from the wall, my nose would be touching the opposite wall. I had never been to jail or a military prison before, but perhaps because it was a place meant to imprison ability users, it was different from a normal prison. Weren’t prisons supposed to be dark? This place was overly bright, with red lasers cutting through the space and lighting it up.

There was supposed to be something like an old iron bed and a toilet here, but there was nothing. Literally nothing. There was nothing on the glossy walls either. There were no light bulbs, so the walls themselves were emitting light, but the light was so intense I thought my eyes were going to fall out. Shouldn’t they be able to slip a shabby meal through a small gap in the door or something? But there were no gaps in the door, as if it had never been opened.

I looked around my surroundings again and was dumbfounded once more. Why should I be held in the same level of confinement as an ability user when I don’t even have an ability? I wouldn’t even be able to escape from a normal prison, so there was no need for them to go this far. I was speechless at the injustice. They didn’t even remove my handcuffs. Even a murderer had no right to be treated like this. I needed to file a complaint with the Human Rights Commission. Such brutality.

My eyes hurt from the light coming from all directions, so I just sat down and buried my face in my knees. It was still bright. After squatting like that for a while, I got hungry. It was funny how I could still get hungry in such a situation, but it had been so long since I had last felt this sensation that I welcomed it. Now that they weren’t forcefully feeding me some moisture, I also felt thirsty. I wanted to chew and eat something, not be supplied with nutrients through my blood. I wanted to drink water from a cup. I wanted to be in a dark place where I couldn’t be seen. I wanted to sleep without passing out.

“Ah... seriously...”

I was dying of thirst, but I couldn’t let the moisture drain from my eyes. If I wanted to hold on a little longer, I couldn’t cry. I was painfully aware that they weren’t human enough to let me cry. I had no sense of time either. I couldn’t believe I had to stay here for a week. I shouldn’t have sworn. No, weren’t they the bad ones for creating a situation where I was forced to swear? Why am I blaming myself?

*Beep, beep, beep, beep, beep—beeeep!*

As I lay on my stomach, it seemed like I had fallen asleep for a bit, but my head lifted at a sudden, ear-piercing noise ringing in my ears. The noise stopped as I looked around.

“I can’t even sleep?”

I was in disbelief at the conduct of this place. If you wanted me to work here so badly, you should’ve treated me well from the start. Or at least treat me well now. There was no way I was going to spend the rest of my life in a place that only ordered and threatened. I vowed to myself. I would never yield.

To my dismay, my willpower was weaker than I thought. All this time, I hadn’t had enough opportunities to test the limits of my will. There were no windows or clocks, so I couldn’t tell how much time had passed, but I felt like I could do just about anything to get out of here now. If only they would just give me a sip of water, if only they would just turn those lights off, and most importantly, if only they would just make it so that the ear-grating noise that would start up at any time and anywhere would not sound out... If I tried to sleep, that high-pitched sound would ring out and dig into my brain, making me scared of closing my eyes. The room, which seemed to be a little over a pyeong[^2] in size, was filled with an unpleasant heat due to the light coming from all directions. If I stayed in the same position for too long, electricity would spew out from the lasers connecting from wall to wall. I sat, stood, and laid down... I felt like I was going insane.

My father had already consented to my being in the S.E. Anyhow, since I couldn’t escape, I had my doubts about the meaning of my time here. Feeling like my eyes were going to fall out, I closed them for a second, and when I heard the sound of a *beep–* again, I made up my mind. I had to get out of here. Even if I had to die, I didn’t want to die crazy in here. The underlying reason for keeping me here must be to make me obedient. Then, wouldn’t it be all over if I acted obediently? Honestly, I wasn’t even rebelling against them. It was a human reaction, but... they didn’t see me as a human.

I pulled myself together and looked around my surroundings. There was CCTV everywhere, so they could probably hear me talking to myself pretty well. The lights that seemed to never go out went out just as I was about to open my mouth. In an instant, darkness fell, and a cool breeze rushed in from somewhere. The door to the outside opened. I hadn’t even said anything, but did those crazy bastards go so far as to hack into my mind? Could it be that mental-type abilities worked on me? Nothing would make me happier.

Then, someone suddenly grabbed my hand and dragged me outside. I didn’t know who it was, but I was desperate to get out, so I followed them. The corridor was as dark as a blackout. I couldn’t even see myself, so I had no way of knowing who was standing in front of me. After turning a few corners in what I assumed was a corridor, I no longer had the strength to walk. I wasn’t sure if it had been a day, two days, or three days, but I hadn’t slept a wink or had any water. I wasn’t the type of ability user who could go months without eating and still function normally.

As I sat down, the person in front of me placed something in my hand. Even though I couldn’t see it, the familiar feel of it told me what it was. A 500-ml plastic bottle. Water. I attempted to unscrew the cap, but my hand continued to turn without any strength. The plastic bottle then vanished from my grasp.

“Ah...”

I hurriedly reached my hand out to grab the plastic bottle again. This time, it didn’t have its cap on. I gulped down the water. The sudden influx of water into my windpipe made me cough, so I couldn’t drink properly, but I kept going nonetheless.

“Are you okay? Let’s go a little further.”

I didn’t know whose voice it was, but the kindness of it made me feel unnecessarily emotional. I struggled to push myself up again.

“Can I switch on a light?”

I nodded my head. I didn’t know if they could see in the dark, but as if they had understood my intentions, a small flashlight flicked on. Only then could I recognise who had brought me here. A man who was overly beautiful even in the dim light, the ability user I had seen at the bank, and the main cause of this.

I shook off the man’s grip on my wrist. However, his fingers gently dug between mine, and he intertwined our fingers. He carefully stroked my fingers and spoke in whispers, “I’m sorry. I was assigned another mission after my vacation, so I had no idea they had locked you up in prison.”

“......”

“I can’t compensate you, but... I can get you out.”

The man grabbed my hand and continued to guide me. I wanted to shake his hand off and curse at him, but if he could actually get me out... I continued to follow him. I just wanted to get out of here. That was all I could think about.

A solid door stood in our way every few metres, but it would open as soon as the man entered the passcode in an accustomed manner. It was almost baffling how easy it was. Perhaps we had walked for a while, or perhaps I was the only one who felt that way, but we eventually came to a corridor with a window. It had been so long since I’d last seen sunlight, and not artificial light, that I stopped in my tracks.

“Are you having difficulty?”

The man opened a small door beside him. Instead of the white walls I had grown tired of and strange machines, a normal room emerged. In front of the television were a small table and a cloth couch. The man sat me down on the couch and rummaged through the fridge. He soon brought out a few puddings that looked like something bought from a convenience store.

“This is the staff break room. I wasn’t the one who bought these puddings, but...”

The man peeled off the cover of the pudding and even brought over a disposable spoon and placed it on the table. As I unconsciously reached for the pudding, the sound of a clink came from my wrists, which were still handcuffed.

“Hold on.”

The man gently grabbed the handcuff chains around my wrists. Despite the fact that he had clearly grabbed it gently, the chains broke with a crack.

“Does your wrist hurt?”

He broke apart the iron wrapped around my wrist as if he were breaking up crackers. I stared, dumbfounded, because I’d never seen a man break iron apart with his bare hands before. It wasn’t as if this were some kind of martial arts novel where iron could be cut with a sword. Soon after, the man scooped up the pudding with the spoon. He then attempted to place it in my mouth.

I summoned all the strength I didn’t have and slapped his hand away. The pudding on the spoon fell to the floor with a clink. It was funny how I was acting proud after following him all the way here, but I couldn’t just eat the pudding spoon fed to me by the root of my problem.

“I’m sorry. I was just happy to meet another ability user outside...”

“......”

“You know, ability users don’t even make up 0.1% of the world population... People need us in times of crisis, but normally they look at us like we’re monsters...”

Know my foot. All this time, I’ve corresponded to the 99.99%. Plus, I was the type of person the man was talking about—someone who thought that ability users were needed in times of crisis but never considered becoming friends with them or otherwise associated with them. It was too early to lump me in the category of “us”. I was embarrassed when he suddenly confessed such heartfelt feelings to me.

“I liked you from the moment I saw you. I brought you here because I thought we could be comrades.”

What comrades? Were we a group of pirates or what? However, the man’s fluttering lashes drooped and cast a shadow. Even his downcast eyes looked slightly wet. His lips slightly trembled as well, giving him a pitiful look.

Seeing the trembling in his eyes made me feel weak, but I couldn’t tell him it was okay. Even though I knew it was an obligation to report ability users to the nation, and even though I knew he didn’t intend for what had happened to me to happen, he was still the definite cause. I wasn’t able to shake off the thought that if it weren’t for him, I’d be going to university unscathed. When I didn’t say anything, he pushed the pudding and spoon into my hand. He gave a small sigh and said, “Can’t you at the very least eat this now?”

Actually, I hadn’t eaten in days, so the chocolate-coloured pudding in front of me was very tempting. Hitting the man’s hand earlier took a lot of willpower. I grabbed the spoon with shaky fingers and scooped up the pudding. However, the slippery pudding kept running down.

“Can’t I scoop it up for you?”

The man looked at me with pitiful eyes. His neat and pretty brow creased slightly, and his well-groomed eyebrows plied. I couldn’t turn weak against the main cause of this situation, but… Honestly, wasn’t the real cause of this situation my throwing a magazine at the robber because of my nosiness? Then wouldn’t I be the one in the wrong, and the man wouldn’t be the cause of this? Still, I was afraid that the man would get hurt. Ah, I don’t know either.

I threw away the spoon I was holding. The spoon didn’t get very far and landed right under the table. The man stood up, went to the drawer next to the sink, and retrieved another disposable spoon. Then, he scooped up the pudding again and placed it in my mouth. This time, he pushed a little harder, and in the confusion of the moment, I let it in my mouth.

Should I spit it out? My rationality pondered it over for a moment, but my instincts swallowed the pudding. As the sweet taste melted on my tongue, my instincts screamed for more, more, more. The man continued to scoop the pudding and place it in my mouth. In no time at all, the cup of pudding was gone. The man casually took the cover off the second pudding. Seeing its yellow colour this time, it seemed to be banana-flavoured. I thought of the first time I met him. At that time, he was wearing a mustard-coloured sweater. I found myself pathetic for having such stupid thoughts even in the midst of all of this, and my entire head hurt. The man brought the pudding to my mouth again. I hesitated before eating it. After having emptied three cups of pudding just like that, my sense of reality slowly returned. Was it okay to just play around here? Weren’t we on the run? Wasn’t this an urgent situation? Was it normal to scarf down three cups of pudding in a break room while being chased?

“Let’s go now.”

As if to answer my question, the man stood up and even helped me to my feet. We stepped back out into the corridor again, but no one was there. Along the way, we encountered a few people standing guard, but the man deftly knocked them out. He even retrieved some everyday clothes and dressed me in them. The smoothness of things heightened my anxiety.

“This is the last stop.”

The man entered the passcode and held his hand over the scanner. A red light scanned the man from head to toe. *Beep!* A warning sound suddenly echoed out. *Bang, bang, bang*, security shutters began to slam down from a distance. The sirens grew louder and louder, and the distant, soldier-like footsteps grew closer.

“It seems like we’ve been caught. I was trying not to cause any property damage, though,” the man said in a small voice, and he put his hand on the door. The door melted. I had already been surprised to see him break the handcuffs, but I was even more surprised to see him melt down such a thick-looking door in an instant.

“Hurry and go.”

The man pushed my back. Obviously, I wanted to leave, but I was worried about the aftermath. My mind felt congested. Meanwhile, wailing sirens could be heard all around me, interrupting my thoughts.

“What about you?”

“I’ll stop them.”

At some point, I began to see soldiers approaching in the distance. No, should I say people similar to soldiers? The man took a gun from his arms and put it in my hand. Then, he pushed my back again. In the confusion of the moment, I went outside. The last security shutter slammed down between the man and me. Covered by the shutters, neither the man nor the people similar to soldiers could be seen.

Since S.E. had a front gate, it probably had a back gate as well, and it seemed like I had broken one and gotten out of one of the old, unused side doors. No, I hadn’t broken it, the man had melted it. I looked around, and the sight of a rugged mountain dense with trees spread out before me made all the high-end facilities I’d seen so far look silly.

I had heard rumours that S.E. was built by carving out a whole mountain. It was said that Mount Achung, where S.E. was located, was a national security zone where civilian entrance was controlled and that even airplanes were not allowed to fly over it. People held a big interest in S.E., but with so little known about it, all sorts of rumours spread out. I wasn’t really interested, so I only listened to them in passing, but it seemed as though the real S.E. was more like a village in a large forest than I had expected. The round walls went on with no apparent end, sideways and upwards. Suddenly, what had happened inside seemed like a dream.

I didn’t know where to go first, so I hiked up a mountain with no path. Looking for a road where people and cars pass by, I realised I didn’t seem like someone running away. I didn’t know how the man who was breaking and melting iron was defending himself, but no one came out of the molten door. My speed wasn’t improving at all, but I figured it would be faster than walking, so I ran. However, I soon ran out of strength to run, so I walked. I walked at such a crawl that I could pull grass from the bare ground, but something was strange. Why was it so easy? It was as if someone was pushing me in the back to run away. Yeah, the man did push me in the back.

In the movies, even if you managed to escape, there’d be a helicopter or something flying around, brightly lighting up the area as it chased after you, but my surroundings were very quiet. I could only hear the sounds of my footsteps, my breathing, birds singing, and leaves rustling in the wind. It was as if nothing was happening. It was to the point where I had no idea what I was doing and no sense of reality. Were they thinking that there was no need to chase me anymore, as if they’d never been guarded against me in the first place? No, maybe they weren’t even guarded against me. If there were that many people out there who could melt iron, then what was the point of handcuffs and prison?

No one was chasing me, but I had to be chased. I didn’t know where to go, and I had no talent whatsoever for running away, but I felt hopeless in front of the reality that I had to move. I grasped the gun in my hand, but I’d never shot it properly before. I’ve had some rough lessons in the past, and I was even shot recently, but I didn’t have the confidence to make an accurate hit. Maybe if I did land an accurate hit on someone, it’d make things worse for me.

As the sun overhead gradually tilted to the west, I heard the sound of a car in the distance. Even as I stood on guard, I hurriedly quickened my footsteps. “If you change the multiple-launch rocket system, you’ll have to change the ballistic missiles too, as well as all the components that go along with it... You must be busy in the future.”

“Well, there’s always work to do, so I’ll be busy. Is Lieutenant General Jung doing well? I haven’t seen him since the banquet last year, but now that our Euijin has even stayed over at his place and owes him...”

“Owe, you say. My father said that he likes Euijin a lot, saying that he was tall and handsome. Now that I’m looking, Euijin resembles you, Father[^6].”

“Haha, you’re the one who resembles the lieutenant general to a T.”

Staying detached from the world they created, I just drank my water. My father and I couldn’t hold a conversation for any longer than five minutes, but he conversed with Haekyung in such a friendly manner that it was hard to believe that this was their first time meeting.

Given that my father was a businessman, in some ways, it was a natural skill for him to be capable of appropriately socialising with those he needed. However, Haekyung’s friendliness and sociability always surprised me. I had thought that Haekyung was amiable, bright, affable, and kind. It made him shine much brighter than his appearance, background, and ability did. However, I was no longer sure about that. I knew what my father’s goal was, but I didn’t know what Haekyung’s goal was. Was that also a part of the information Director Baek had told him under the pretext of keeping an eye on me? My father only spoke of him, even when Haekyung left his spot for a bit.

“I was super worried when you first came here, but it seems that was all for naught. How did you become close with Haekyung?”

“...Haekyung has always... been very social...”

“It’s nice how this place feels more like a university campus than the army. What’s your rank?”

“I’m still a recruit.”

“And Haekyung?”

“...You already know.”

“......”

“......”

“How did Lieutenant General Jung raise his son so well? Isn’t Haekyung’s ability the most outstanding one here?”

“......”

“What did you say your ability was?”

“...It’s a confidential matter.”

“How was Lieutenant General Jung when you saw him?”

“I don’t know.”

With that as a starting point, my father and I didn’t exchange any more words until Haekyung returned. We probably won’t be meeting for a while now. I was tired of this meeting.

It was not only chilly at night, but also cold now. I spent the evening with Haekyung today as well, and we went to the park for a while, where we sat on a bench together. As the cold wind made my body huddle into itself a bit, Haekyung carefully adjusted my clothes and wrapped his arm around my shoulder. In reaction to his gesture full of aegyo as he clung to my body, I found myself smiling without even realising it. However, my smile faded at his following words.

“Let’s go to my house for dinner this Saturday.”

“What?”

“My mom invited you over.”

“I’m fine.’”

“Let’s go over to my house on the last Saturday of this month. I felt bad seeing you go home alone too, so this is good, isn’t it?”

“No, I already said I’m fine...”

“My family likes you. Noona said that you seemed really chic in an indifferent sort of way, or something along those lines.”

Jung Haekyung started again. Typically, in situations like this, I tend to go along with his opinion for the most part. However, I had strong, unpleasant memories associated with Haekyung’s home, so I really didn’t want to go. I braced myself and firmly said, “No. I’m not going.”

“Why?”

What should I say to convince him? No, whatever I say, Haekyung wouldn’t be convinced. I just decided to say what I had to say.

“It’s uncomfortable. You even witnessed how I got indigestion back then.”

“Then, should we invite your father too?”

I was taken aback by Haekyung’s completely unexpected words, and he smiled like he did the day we first met.

“You said that you feel comfortable when it’s just my family. It’ll be nice to have a bride and bridegroom family meeting together, won’t it?”

Haekyung took out his phone, as if he were really going to contact him. He had already exchanged phone numbers with my father during the last visit. If Haekyung made the call, it was obvious my father would attend the dinner, no matter what he had going on. I hastily grabbed Haekyung’s hand.

“My father is overseas right now, though.”

“I know.”

Haekyung smiled again. Although it seemed a little too strange to just chalk it up to him worrying about a lonely friend, something about it had seemed weird to me for quite a while now, so it was difficult to find the words I had to say.

“He’s busy, so... don’t call him.”

“But I called him yesterday? He said he had just finished something urgent. You should call him first sometimes too, you unfilial son.”

“Yesterday?”

“Yeah. I called my father, so while I was at it, I called my father-in-law as well.”

My mind was filled with words before they vanished like a blank piece of paper. Now that it had come to this, I decided to take a frontal stance when speaking again. I felt sorry for Haekyung, and even though he probably wouldn’t listen, I had to express my opinion. I couldn’t just let him contact my father either.

“Thanks for thinking of me, but I don’t want to go to your house. Don’t contact my father either.”

I chose my words carefully, afraid that Haekyung would ask me why again. However, he responded strangely.

“I want to have dinner with you at my house.”

I was the one who asked why this time. Haekyung answered, “Because you don’t want to, so there’s no helping it.”

“...What do you mean?”

“It’s just as I said. Because you don’t want to, I’m going to have dinner with you. At my house.”

“Why?”

“I wonder if our Euijin is naive, pure, or just dumb.”

“Please, just say something that makes sense!”

“No matter how much I think about it, you’re a little dumb, just as Baek Cheongah had said. You don’t even know about my feelings.”

“Hey!”

“In order to return from a place you don’t want to go to, you’d have to give me a kiss, won’t you?”

“......”

“In order to return from a place...”

“...Stop it.”

“I don’t want to do anything you dislike either. So, you should try and be good all the time.”

Was Haekyung starting another one of his jokes again? I didn’t have the energy to play along with this joke. At times like this, Haekyung would just start to utter strange things, and I, being as dumb as Haekyung had said, couldn’t understand them. I said in a voice that was nearly inaudible, “I don’t know what you’re talking about. Why would we... do something like that?”

“We did it last time.”

“That was... You were just joking around.”

“Our Euijin seems to have become quite open-minded after living abroad. Two guys kissing is just a joke?”

“......”

“Our Euijin seems…”

“It is a joke! Didn’t you say it was just a greeting?”

When I couldn’t hold it in anymore and shouted, Haekyung laughed. *Then, give me a greeting.* He had said this while tapping his lips.

“Haekyung-ah... why are you suddenly acting like this again?”

“Suddenly? I’ve wanted to kiss you since the moment I first saw you.”

“...What?”

“Why are you acting as though you don’t feel that way as well? You fell for me at first sight too.”

“...No...”

“No? You saved me.”

“...That was... It wasn’t much help anyway...”

“You risked your life, completely unaware of your own ability, to protect me. That was the moment I fell for you.”

“...I, I didn’t.”

“You said I was cool too. That you liked me.”

It was true that I liked Haekyung. It was also true that I thought he was cool. But I didn’t like Haekyung in the way he thought I did. There were so many things I had to say that I didn’t even know what to start with, but I couldn’t just not answer him. I didn’t want to hear him repeat the same words again.

“I’m sorry...”

Even if it was by force, since I ended up opening my mouth, my words burst out as if the dam had been torn. I continued to speak in mumbles.

“I’m sorry if the way I kept glancing at you made you feel upset. For me, it was just like admiring a piece of art... No, I’m sorry. That could upset you. I won’t do it anymore. I’m serious...”

“It doesn’t upset me.”

“...Then why are you being like this?”

“No, now that I think of it, your saying sorry does upset me. What’s there to be sorry about?”

“Because I... acted like I liked you. Isn’t that what’s upsetting you? The reason you’re acting like this. But I don’t like you in the way that I want to kiss you...”

“I already told you it didn’t upset me, didn’t I?”

“...Anyhow, I’m sorry for making you misunderstand. So, please, don’t be like this.”

“Misunderstand?”

Since the end of his sentence went up in tone, was this a question as well? If it was a question, I had to answer it. My lips felt dry. I nodded my head and apologised again. After a moment had passed, Haekyung opened his mouth.

“Then, do you not like me?”

This wasn’t a problem that could be divided in such a black-and-white way, like “I like you” or “I don’t.” No, honestly, I liked Haekyung. However, it wasn’t the “like” that he was thinking of. I steeled myself, thinking that the most urgent thing right now was resolving this misunderstanding, and said, “Yeah. I don’t like you.”

“I like you, though.”

I blankly stared in response to the sullen voice I had never heard before. He’d been saying strange things like “Kiss me” or “I fell for you” since earlier, and now he was saying that he liked me? Jung Haekyung? Not just as a friend? I asked again just to be sure, “You like me… in a romantic kind of way?”

“Yeah. I want to go out with you.”

Was the conversation we were having up until this point perhaps Jung Haekyung’s confession? Upon hearing his words and the overly natural way he answered them, memories of his past actions flitted through my mind. When I thought about it, I could somewhat understand his behaviour. If he thought of that as some kind of fling... Crazy, a fling. A chill spread throughout my body.

I was left speechless. The limitless amount of time I was suddenly bestowed this time around honestly made me feel even more nervous. My heart felt anxious about having to reject Haekyung. Still, I couldn’t accept his confession. It wasn’t because he was a man. Although that was actually a very important issue, there was something of even bigger importance, so it slipped my mind. Jung Haekyung was weird. Something about him was very, no, *too* weird. Even if his behaviour was said to be driven by his feelings for me, I couldn’t accept them based on my personal standards.

Honestly, there had been some parts that made it difficult for us to remain even as friends. But to be lovers in the midst of all that? Even if I tried to honestly think about it, it made my spine run cold. Even though I liked Haekyung, even though he helped me a lot when I was having a hard time, and even though I had no intention of ending my friendship with him or stopping seeing him despite the fact he was a little weird... That was all because we were friends. They were problems that I could overlook because we were friends.

I chose to hurt Haekyung rather than give him false hope. If this was Haekyung’s confession, albeit rather twisted and foul, it was only right to reject it. I was a little scared of rejecting him, but my feelings of apology took precedence. Maybe he won’t even want to see me anymore. Still, I couldn’t accept such a strange confession. Honestly, I didn’t even want to take it slow and think it over again.

“I’ve never thought of you as anything more than a friend.”

“......”

“I’m sorry.”

I dropped my head down. I didn’t have the guts to face Haekyung. How could I dare to reject Jung Haekyung? But could this even be called a rejection? Could that even be called a confession? Either way, I didn’t regret anything. Rather, I felt a bit relieved knowing that the questions I had harboured up until now were resolved, even if it was just a little bit. Come to think of it, I was the one who made him misunderstand. It was true that I’d been mesmerised by his face since the very start. I was definitely sorry for that.

“I understand,” Haekyung meekly said. I felt like it’d be rude to constantly apologise, so I just nodded my head. It didn’t seem like I should get up first, so I waited until Haekyung stood up. However, a while later, I heard some strange words coming from above my head again.

“Yes, Father. This is Haekyung. Of course. Nothing much. Euijin has also been doing well. Oh, just because… When will you be arriving in Korea?”

I raised my head. I locked eyes with Jung Haekyung. Haekyung continued the call while staring at me.

“My father said that he would like to have a meal with you. Yes, although it may not seem like it, Euijin wants to see you too… It’s this Saturday. Is it too sudden? Really? Then, please call me again tomorrow.”

Haekyung hung up the call. I continued to stare at him.

“The schedule’s already been confirmed, so you’ll come, won’t you? You should have a meal with the lieutenant general.”

“...What are you doing? You literally just said you understood.”

“Yeah, that’s why I called. Because you don’t like me, but I like you.”

“...What?”

“A peck or a kiss or whatever, it’s fine if I do it first, but… Someone going ahead and kissing someone who doesn’t like them back isn’t a relationship, is it? It’s a crime.”

“......”

“The opposite is the start of a romantic relationship. I want our relationship to start in a romantic way, Euijin-ah.”

“......”

“I’ve heard that communication is important between lovers. In the future, you should always answer me.”

Haekyung grabbed my hand and laughed. Seeing him smile as warmly as he did before, the rumours about Jung Haekyung that I had tried so hard to ignore resurfaced.

He was beautiful, strong, and... cruel.
