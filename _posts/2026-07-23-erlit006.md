When I opened the fridge, a post-it note Jung Haekyung had left the morning he departed fluttered down to the floor.

[Euijin-ah♡ I’m going to spank you if you starve yourself and carelessly sleep on the couch while I’m away. I’ll be back soon. Even if you miss me, please endure it a little longer♡♡♡]

I didn’t miss Jung Haekyung. I had just woken up from a dream where I was dreaming of him anyway. If I fall asleep, I’ll probably dream of him again. Regardless of whether he was by my side, I’d always see him to a tiring extent, so there was no time for me to miss him.
