[^1]: Classic Korean fairy tale about a woodcutter and a fairy. The fairies usually descended to a pond in the human world to bathe, and they’d remove their celestial robes, which allowed them to fly back up to heavens, to soak in the water. Having been informed by a deer he saved about the whereabouts of the pond, he steals the robes of one of the fairies and so that he could ‘rescue’ her and take her as his wife. However, he wasn’t supposed to tell her about this until they had at least three children. The woodcutter was impatient however and told her when they had two and showed her the robes. The fairy decided she wanted to go back home and took her children with her to fly back to the heavens. There’s more to this story, but this is the context for what Haekyung is referencing here. Versions may vary, this is what my bad memory remembers of the version I was told lol unlike the woodcutter, who gave her the clothes after she gave him two children, Haekyung will only give Euijin the clothes if he gives him four children, making sure he can’t escape.
